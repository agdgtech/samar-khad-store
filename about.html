About SAMAR KHAD STORE

A local fertiliser store on Ratupura Road, Sambhal, run by Mohd Adnan.

Our story

Serving the farmers of Sambhal

SAMAR KHAD STORE was started so that farmers in Ratupura and nearby villages can buy quality fertilisers close to home at fair prices, along with honest advice on how to use them.

We stock nitrogenous, NPK / complex and organic fertilisers, and every sale comes with a proper GST invoice.
