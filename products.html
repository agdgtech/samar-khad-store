Tips

Getting the most from your fertiliser

1. Test your soil

A soil health card shows which nutrients your field lacks, so you only buy what you need.

2. Split the dose

Apply nitrogen in two or three splits instead of all at once to reduce losses and feed the crop when it needs it.

3. Mix with organic

Use organic manure along with chemical fertilisers to keep the soil healthy over the long term.
